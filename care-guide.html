Aluminum Tumbler

Care Guide

Cleaning

Aluminum Tumbler is dishwasher safe. Place it on the top rack for best results. The powder coat finish is designed to hold up to regular machine washing without fading or chipping.

For hand washing, use warm water and mild dish soap. Avoid abrasive scrubbers or steel wool, which can scratch the finish over time.

DishwasherSafe — top rack recommended
Hand WashWarm water, mild soap
AvoidAbrasive scrubbers, steel wool, bleach-based cleaners

The Straw

The included drinking straw is also dishwasher safe. Run it through with your cups or rinse it by hand after each use.

For a thorough clean, use a straw brush (not included) to remove any residue from the inside. This is especially useful if you use the cup for smoothies, juices, or thicker drinks.

Finish & Color

The powder coat finish on each cup is durable and fade-resistant under normal use. To keep the color looking its best, avoid soaking the cup in standing water for extended periods and store it in a dry location.

Minor scuffs from everyday use are normal and do not affect the performance of the cup. Deep scratches that expose bare aluminum should be kept dry to prevent oxidation.

What to Avoid

MicrowaveDo not microwave — aluminum is not microwave safe
BleachAvoid bleach or chlorine-based cleaners
OvenNot oven safe
Prolonged soakingAvoid leaving submerged in water for hours at a time
