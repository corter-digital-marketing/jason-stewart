Packaging

Every order ships in a custom, reusable box lined with natural cotton — designed to be kept, not discarded. The cup is wrapped in tissue and seated in a molded insert. A care card is included with each order.

If you would like to include a handwritten note, add the message in the order notes at checkout. There is no additional charge for this.

Damaged in Transit

If your order arrives damaged, please photograph the packaging and the piece and contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you.

Contact: info@thejasonstewartcollection.com
